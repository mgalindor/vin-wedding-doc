Our Story

A love that started
over a cup of coffee

It was a rainy Tuesday in 2019 when Emma walked into James's favorite bookshop-café in Roma Norte. She asked for the last copy of a novel they both wanted; he offered to share it. Five years, two cities, and a golden retriever named Biscuit later, we are ready to say yes — forever.

We can't wait to celebrate this moment with the people who have walked with us through every chapter. Thank you for being part of our story.
